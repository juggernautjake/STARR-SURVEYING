# 14 · Property in El Paso

**Who is calling** — Nine hours away. Almost certainly not a job we can take.

**What this is testing** — The agent must not promise service it cannot deliver, and must not refuse on its own authority either. Take the details, be honest that distance is a question for Hank.

**Roughly** 2 minutes · **awkward**

---

## The call

**1.** “Hi, I'm looking for a surveyor for a property in El Paso. Ten acres out past Canutillo. Do y'all cover that area?”

   > 👀 It must not say yes. It must also not say no. "I am not sure how far we go — let me get your details and have Hank call you" is right.

**2.** “Okay, sure.”

**3.** “Antonia Escamilla. E-S-C-A-M-I-L-L-A. 915-555-0187.”

**4.** “It's undeveloped, nothing on it. My uncle left it to me.”

**5.** “I need to know the boundaries before I sell it.”

**6.** “aescamilla at gmail dot com.”

*This is the list that matters. An agent that gathers everything by asking about
everything has failed at the only thing separating it from a form.*
