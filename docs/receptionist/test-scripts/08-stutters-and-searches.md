# 08 · Stutters, pauses, and keeps going to find things

**Who is calling** — Delbert Oyelaran, elderly, unhurried, keeps leaving the phone to look for paperwork.

**What this is testing** — Long silences that are NOT the caller having hung up. The agent must wait, must not talk over him, and must not treat a pause as a refusal.

**Roughly** 5 minutes · **hard**

---

## The call

**1.** “Hello? Hello. Yes, I'm — I'm calling about, uh, about getting my— my land surveyed. I've got— hold on—”

   *(…then go quiet for 11 seconds before carrying on)*

   > 👀 Eleven seconds of nothing mid-message. It must not cut him off or jump in.

**2.** “Sorry. Sorry about that. My— my name is Delbert. Delbert Oyelaran. I don't— I can never remember my own phone number, hold on, let me—”

   *(…then go quiet for 18 seconds before carrying on)*

**3.** “Two five four. Five five five. Oh one four two. I think. Yes. 254-555-0142.”

   > 👀 He gave it haltingly and then confirmed. This should be one number, not three fragments.

**4.** “Yes, yes. Go ahead. I'm not— I'm not in a hurry.”

**5.** “It's O— O-Y-E-L-A-R-A-N. Oyelaran. My father was Nigerian. Delbert is just— it's D-E-L-B-E-R-T.”

**6.** “The place is out on— it's off of, uh— hold on, it's on the tax thing—”

   *(…then go quiet for 22 seconds before carrying on)*

   > 👀 Twenty-two seconds. The longest pause in any script. This is where a badly built agent gives up on him.

**7.** “County Road 3300. Out past Gatesville. I don't— I don't know the number on it.”

**8.** “It's about— I want to say eight acres? It might be nine. The tax paper says.”

**9.** “There's the old house and a— there's a well house. And a barn, but the barn fell in.”

**10.** “My daughter says I need it surveyed before I— before I put it in the trust.”

**11.** “I don't have— I don't do the email. My daughter does. Can I have her call you?”

**12.** “Alright. Well. Thank you for being patient with me.”

**13.** “No, that's— no. Thank you. Bye now.”

---

## It should end up with

- firstName Delbert
- lastName Oyelaran spelled
- phone 254-555-0142
- propertyAddress County Road 3300 past Gatesville
- acreage 8 or 9 acres, uncertain
- structures house, well house, collapsed barn
- surveyPurpose putting land in a trust
- email none — daughter handles it

## Notes

The single most important script. If the agent survives this one it will survive most real callers. Watch for: interrupting, repeating a question he already answered slowly, or recording "eight acres" as certain when he said it might be nine.
