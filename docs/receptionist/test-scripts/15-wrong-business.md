# 15 · Wanted a different kind of surveyor

**Who is calling** — Looking for a building surveyor to inspect a roof. Wrong trade entirely.

**What this is testing** — Recognising that this is not a job at all, and ending the call quickly and kindly instead of collecting eleven fields about a roof.

**Roughly** 1 minute · **awkward**

---

## The call

**1.** “Hi, I need a surveyor to come look at my roof — there's some damage and the insurance wants a report on the structure. Can you do that?”

**2.** “Oh. Huh. So you do land, not buildings.”

**3.** “No, no, that's alright. I'll look for a structural engineer then. Thanks anyway.”

---

## Notes

If the agent starts asking for acreage here it has not understood the call at all. Under a minute, politely.
