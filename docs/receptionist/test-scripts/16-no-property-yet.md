# 16 · Under contract, has not closed

**Who is calling** — Buying 40 acres, option period ends Friday, does not own it yet.

**What this is testing** — Urgency plus an ownership answer that is neither yes nor no. The deadline must survive into the record.

**Roughly** 3 minutes · **awkward**

---

## The call

**1.** “Hi — I'm under contract on forty acres in Lampasas County and my option period ends Friday. I need a survey fast or I have to decide blind. Nathan Oduya, 512-555-0144.”

   > 👀 FRIDAY is the most important word in this call. If it does not reach the record this call has failed.

**2.** “Yes, quickly please.”

**3.** “O-D-U-Y-A. Nathan, normal spelling.”

**4.** “It's off CR 2210 outside Kempner. The listing says 40.2 acres.”

**5.** “I'm not the owner yet — I'm the buyer. It closes in three weeks if I go through with it.”

   > 👀 Neither owner nor agent. Watch how this is recorded.

**6.** “There's an old hunting cabin on it and a couple of deer blinds, that's it.”

**7.** “I've got the listing, the survey from 2004, and the title commitment.”

**8.** “noduya at gmail dot com. N-O-D-U-Y-A.”

**9.** “I'll email them over right now. Please, if he could call me today.”

**10.** “Thank you.”

## It should NEVER have asked about

*This is the list that matters. An agent that gathers everything by asking about
everything has failed at the only thing separating it from a form.*

## Notes

Deer blinds are structures, barely. Either answer is defensible; what matters is that the cabin is recorded.
