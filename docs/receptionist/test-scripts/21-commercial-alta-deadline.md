# 21 · Commercial ALTA with a closing date

**Who is calling** — Title company closer. Precise, fast, knows exactly what she needs.

**What this is testing** — A professional caller who will be annoyed by basic questions. Everything is in the first forty seconds.

**Roughly** 2 minutes · **straightforward**

---

## The call

**1.** “Hi, Karen Stubblefield at Greenline Title, 512-555-0142. We need an ALTA/NSPS land title survey for a commercial closing — 1420 Elm Street, Georgetown. It's a retail building with a parking lot, about 1.8 acres. Closing is the 14th so we need it back by the 9th. I've got the title commitment and the legal description ready to send.”

   > 👀 Everything is here. The agent should ask almost nothing — maybe her email and the spelling of her surname.

**2.** “Yes.”

**3.** “S-T-U-B-B-L-E-F-I-E-L-D.”

**4.** “kstubblefield at greenlinetitle dot com.”

**5.** “Perfect, I'll send it over in the next ten minutes. Thanks.”

---

## It should end up with

- firstName Karen
- lastName Stubblefield spelled
- phone
- email
- propertyAddress 1420 Elm Street, Georgetown
- acreage 1.8
- structures retail building and parking lot
- surveyType ALTA/NSPS
- surveyPurpose commercial closing
- DEADLINE back by the 9th, closing the 14th
- callerRole title company
- documents title commitment and legal description

## It should NEVER have asked about

- structures
- acreage
- surveyType
- documents
- callerRole
- propertyAddress

*This is the list that matters. An agent that gathers everything by asking about
everything has failed at the only thing separating it from a form.*

## Notes

If this call takes more than about ninety seconds, the agent is asking a professional things she already told it. That is how you lose a repeat commercial client.
