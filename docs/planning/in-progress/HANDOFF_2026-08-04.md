## 2. What shipped 2026-08-04

### CAD — six slices, every one found by driving a browser

| Slice | Finding |
|---|---|
| **S8c** | The research import **created features on layers that did not exist.** Dialog said "3 feature(s) will be added", they were added, canvas stayed empty. `getVisibleFeatures` drops any feature whose layer is missing. Both halves were individually correct; only the *composition* was wrong, which no module's own tests can see. |
| **S8d** | Imported tracts landed **off the white sheet** — a traverse south of the POB has negative northings while the paper occupies y ≥ 0. Fixed by moving the **paper**, never the geometry. |
| **S11** | Title-block text: wrapping sliced mid-word ("Texas State Plan / e"), and **pixel font floors** meant boxes shrank with zoom while lettering did not — at 8% zoom labels piled up outside a thumbnail sheet. |
| **S12** | `CADErrorBoundary` *promised* "your most recent auto-save will be offered" and **never wrote one**. Exit paths were already covered; the gap was crashes with the page still open. |
| **S13** | **On a fresh drawing, nothing you drew appeared.** Correct live readout, then no line; Select All reported "3 SELECTED" over an empty canvas. |
| **S16a / S17** | A **mislabelled Texas zone** (2277 is Central, not North Central) hardcoded in five places; and the survey-data import now frames the sheet it just centred. |

**S13 is the one to remember.** The bug was *already known and already fixed — in the wrong places.*
`newDocument()` carries a comment describing this exact orphaning; the fix went there and to
`loadDocument`, and **not to the initial state**, which is the path that runs when you simply open the
editor. It survived because every test called `newDocument()` first — testing its way *around* the
broken path.

**And the first fix for it was wrong**, caught by the owner: it seeded `layerOrder[0]`, which is
`SURVEY-INFO`, the reserved title-block layer. The rule is not "always have an active layer" but
**never create geometry the surveyor cannot see, and never choose the layer for them.**

### Finance / tax / intake — a new program, seven slices

`FINANCE_TAX_AND_INTAKE_2026-08-04.md`. F1 card registry, F2 pass-through recovery, F3 tax summaries
(+ F3b on screen), F4 bulk receipt capture, F5 invoice-anyone, F6 intake verified and guarded.

Two modelling ideas worth keeping:

- **A match on `last4` is a guess.** Every outcome needs review, including the single match — auto-
  applying one is how a charge on an unregistered card inherits someone else's tax treatment.
- **A partial recovery is not no-net-gain.** Pay a sanitarian $450, bill $400, and a boolean says
  "nets to zero" while the job lost $50. Recovery is arithmetic with a signed delta, no tolerance
  band.

## 5. Two things worth carrying forward

- **A probe can manufacture a dead site.** Marion failed `fetch()` twice and would have been written
  off as unreachable; in a real browser it loads fine. *"We could not reach it"* is itself a claim.
  The 2026-08-04 counterpart: Playwright "could not reach localhost" for a whole session. It could —
  `AUTH_URL` pointed at dead port 3000, so every middleware redirect hung forever.
- **A county can contradict itself.** San Augustine's banner says its index starts 1800, its welcome
  text says 1856, and a real 1838 deed came back. Both claims are recorded and neither resolved.
