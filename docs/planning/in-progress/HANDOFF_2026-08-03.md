## 3. What this session did

### County coverage: 22 → 41 routed counties

**S-7 was not the slice it looked like.** `texaslandrecords.com` is a *directory*, not a records
system — its 22 county links point at the `uslandrecords.com` portals already driven, plus three
Kofile ones. There is nothing to parse and no adapter to write.

What it publishes is the county → subdomain mapping R39 said had to be found one county at a time,
which is why the Avenu adapter served **two** counties. So the slice became a county-list expansion
and the parser needed no change:

- **Avenu 2 → 19 counties**; two new subdomains (`i2m`, `i2g`) beyond the known `i2i`/`i2j` pair.
- **2 new Kofile counties** — Cochran, Live Oak, both checked for a *Real Property* department
  first, because Williamson taught that a Kofile portal answering 200 may index only Commissioners
  Court.
- Driven before being listed: Val Verde (i2g) → 313 rows; San Augustine (i2m) → 44 rows back to 1838.

### Twelve counties were filed under the wrong FIPS code

Kimble under Kerr's, Menard under Matagorda's, Rockwall under Rusk's, and nine more across the
Henschen, iDocket and Fidlar tables. Third appearance of a bug already fixed by hand once (Lampasas
under La Salle). It is wrong in two directions at once — the intended county silently has no adapter,
and an unrelated county is reported as covered by a portal that is not its own — and
`paid-platform-registry` builds `coveredFIPS` straight from those sets, so it was answering "a
platform covers Kerr" and "nothing covers Kimble".

Pinned by `worker/src/__tests__/fips-labels-match-county-table.test.ts`.

### S-13/S-14: the dedup rule now runs where money is spent

S-11 and S-12 shipped as modules with **zero callers**. The purchase step was still deduping on an
exact raw-instrument match against the ledger, which cannot match across vendors, cannot match
Avenu's records at all (no instrument numbers), and only knows what was *bought* — so a document the
free pass already returned did not stop the paid purchase of the same one.

Free documents are now registered before any paid source is queried, every purchase consults
`decide()`, and the report states both skipped-as-held and bought-under-uncertainty.

**A watermarked preview is seen, not held** — registering one would end a run with a watermarked
image standing in for a clean one and nothing saying a document was missing.

### Phase 9 could never buy anything

The budget guard parsed `"$6-12"` as **$612** (it stripped non-digits and glued the ends of the range
together). Against the default $25 budget every recommendation was unaffordable, so Phase 9 logged
`Budget exceeded` and bought nothing — a bug wearing the costume of a deliberate spending limit.

## 6. Two things worth carrying forward

- **A probe can manufacture a dead site.** Marion failed `fetch()` twice and would have been written
  off as unreachable; in a real browser it loads fine — it bounces through
  `?AspxAutoDetectCookieSupport=1`, which Node's fetch does not survive. The rule here is that a
  county is listed only because its portal answered — but that rule is only as good as the instrument
  doing the asking. "We could not reach it" is itself a claim.
- **A county can contradict itself.** San Augustine's banner says its index starts 1800, its welcome
  text says 1856, and a real 1838 deed came back. Both claims are recorded and neither resolved; a
  search between them is told the county disagrees with itself rather than handed a confident answer.
